及 $S''(x_j - 0) = \frac{2}{h_{j-1}} m_{j-1} + \frac{4}{h_{j-1}} m_j - \frac{6}{h_{j-1}^2}(y_j - y_{j-1})$.

由条件 $S''(x_j + 0) = S''(x_j - 0)$ ($j = 1, 2, \dots, n-1$)，可得
$$\frac{1}{h_{j-1}} m_{j-1} + 2\left(\frac{1}{h_{j-1}} + \frac{1}{h_j}\right) m_j + \frac{1}{h_j} m_{j+1}$$
$$= 3\left(\frac{y_{j+1} - y_j}{h_j^2} + \frac{y_j - y_{j-1}}{h_j^2}\right) (j = 1, 2, \dots, n-1),$$
(2.8.8)

用 $\frac{1}{h_{j-1}} + \frac{1}{h_j}$ 除全式，并注意 $y_j = f_j, \frac{y_{j+1} - y_j}{h_j} = f[x_j, x_{j+1}]$，方程(2.8.8)可简化为
$$\lambda_j m_{j-1} + 2m_j + \mu_j m_{j+1} = g_j \quad (j = 1, 2, \dots, n-1),$$
(2.8.9)

其中 $\lambda_j = \frac{h_j}{h_{j-1} + h_j}, \mu_j = \frac{h_{j-1}}{h_{j-1} + h_j} \quad (j = 1, 2, \dots, n-1),$ (2.8.10)
$$g_j = 3(\lambda_j f[x_{j-1}, x_j] + \mu_j f[x_j, x_{j+1}]) \quad (j = 1, 2, \dots, n-1).$$
(2.8.11)

方程(2.8.9)是关于未知数 $m_0, m_1, \dots, m_n$ 的 $n-1$ 个方程，若加上已知条件(2.8.3)：$m_0 = f'_0$ 则 $m_n = f'_n$，那么方程(2.8.9)为只含 $m_1, \dots, m_{n-1}$ 的 $n-1$ 个方程，写成矩阵形式便是
$$\begin{bmatrix}
2 & \mu_1 & 0 & \dots & \dots & 0 \\
\lambda_2 & 2 & \mu_2 & \ddots & \vdots & \vdots \\
0 & \lambda_3 & 2 & \mu_3 & \ddots & \vdots \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \ddots & \ddots & 0 \\
0 & \vdots & \ddots & 0 & \lambda_{n-1} & 2 \\
0 & \dots & \dots & 0 & \lambda_{n-1} & 2
\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}
m_1 \\
m_2 \\
m_3 \\
\vdots \\
m_{n-2} \\
m_{n-1}
\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
g_1 - \lambda_1 f'_0 \\
g_2 \\
g_3 \\
\vdots \\
g_{n-2} \\
g_{n-1} - \mu_{n-1} f'_n
\end{bmatrix}.$$
(2.8.12)

如果边界条件为式(2.8.4)，则
$$\begin{cases}
2m_0 + m_1 = 3f[x_0, x_1] - \frac{h_0}{2} f''_0 = g_0, \\
m_{n-1} + 2m_n = 3f[x_{n-1}, x_n] + \frac{h_{n-1}}{2} f''_n = g_n.
\end{cases}$$
(2.8.13)

如果边界条件为式(2.8.4)'，则
$$\begin{cases}
2m_0 + m_1 = 3f[x_0, x_1] = g_0, \\
m_{n-1} + 2m_n = 3f[x_{n-1}, x_n] = g_n.
\end{cases}$$
(2.8.13)'

\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}
m_0 \\
m_1 \\
m_2 \\
\vdots \\
m_{n-1} \\

\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}

g_1 \\
g_2 \\
\vdots \\
g_{n-1} \\
g_n
\end{bmatrix}.$$
